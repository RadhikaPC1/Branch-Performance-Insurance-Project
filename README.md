📊 Insurance Analysis: Branch Performance & Sales Dashboard
🔹 Overview

This project focuses on developing a unified analytics solution to monitor branch performance, track sales KPIs, and enable data-driven decision-making in the insurance domain.

The dashboard consolidates data from multiple sources to provide real-time visibility into business metrics, improving reporting efficiency and strategic insights.

🔹 Problem Statement

The organization lacked a centralized system to track and compare branch-level performance across different business streams (New, Cross-sell, Renewal).

Key Challenges:
No real-time tracking of branch KPIs
Fragmented data across Excel sheets and CRM systems
Limited visibility into sales funnel and conversion ratios
Difficulty identifying top vs. underperforming branches
Delayed and inefficient reporting
🎯 Project Objective

To build an interactive dashboard that:

Centralizes data from multiple sources
Automates KPI tracking
Enables real-time business insights
Supports strategic decision-making
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
MySQL – Data extraction and querying
Power BI – Dashboard development & KPI tracking
Tableau – Data visualization & trend analysis
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and transformation

📂 Data Sources
Target Sheets
Brokerage & Fees Data
Invoice Reports
Meetings Data
CRM Opportunities

📊 Key Features
📌 Branch Performance Monitoring
Tracks revenue targets vs. achievement
Performance analysis at branch and individual level
📌 Sales Funnel Analysis
Monitors opportunity pipeline, stages, and win ratios
Evaluates conversion efficiency

Policy Count
Customer Count
Claim Amount
Premium Amount
Payment Success Rate

📈 Key Insights
Total Policy Count: 5,000 and Customer Base: 3,148, indicating a well-distributed portfolio
Policies are evenly split across Active (33.64%), Lapsed (32.8%), and Terminated (33.56%), highlighting retention challenges
Payment Success Rate: 61.81%, with ~38% failures, indicating gaps in payment collection processes
Total Claim Amount (₹251M) significantly exceeds Total Premium (₹5.26M), indicating high claim exposure
Health insurance contributes the highest premium (~1.36M), followed by Life and Property

👥 Customer Insights
Gender distribution is nearly equal:
Female: 32.48%
Male: 33.54%
Other: 33.98%
Highest policy concentration in 60+ age group (1888 policies)
Lower adoption in younger segments, indicating growth opportunity

📉 Trend Analysis
Premium growth shows high fluctuations over time
Indicates inconsistent revenue patterns
Highlights need for better forecasting and planning strategies

🚀 Business Impact
Improved reporting efficiency by ~30% through centralized dashboards
Enabled real-time tracking of KPIs and performance metrics
Identified critical gaps in payment success and claim exposure
Supported data-driven decision-making for business optimization

💡 Recommendations
Implement automated alerts for policy lapses to improve retention
Improve payment collection workflows to reduce failure rates
Use dashboards for weekly performance monitoring
Align individual KPIs with branch-level targets
