📊 Insurance Analysis: Branch Performance & Sales Dashboard

🔹 Overview
This project focuses on building a unified analytics solution to monitor branch performance, track key KPIs, and enable data-driven decision-making in the insurance domain.
The dashboard integrates data from multiple sources to provide real-time visibility, improving reporting efficiency and business insights.

🔹 Problem Statement
The organization lacked a centralized system to track and compare branch-level performance across business streams (New, Cross-sell, Renewal).

⚠️ Key Challenges
No real-time tracking of branch KPIs
Fragmented data across Excel sheets and CRM systems
Limited visibility into sales funnel and conversion ratios
Difficulty identifying top vs. underperforming branches
Delayed and inefficient reporting process
🎯 Project Objective

To design and develop an interactive dashboard that:
Centralizes multi-source data
Automates KPI tracking
Provides real-time insights
Supports strategic decision-making

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
MySQL – Data extraction and querying
Power BI – Dashboard development and KPI tracking
Tableau – Data visualization and trend analysis
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, transformation, and modeling

📂 Data Sources
Target Sheets
Brokerage & Fees Data
Invoice Reports
Meetings Data
CRM Opportunities

📊 Key Features
📌 Branch Performance Monitoring
Tracks revenue targets vs. actual performance
Enables analysis at branch and individual level

📌 Sales Funnel Analysis
Monitors opportunity pipeline, stages, and win ratios
Evaluates conversion efficiency

📌 KPI Dashboard
Policy Count
Customer Count
Claim Amount
Premium Amount
Payment Success Rate

📈 Key Insights
Total Policy Count: 5,000 and Customer Base: 3,148, indicating a well-distributed portfolio
Policies are evenly split across Active (33.64%), Lapsed (32.8%), and Terminated (33.56%), highlighting retention challenges
Payment Success Rate: 61.81%, with ~38% failures, indicating inefficiencies in the payment collection process
Total Claim Amount (₹251M) significantly exceeds Total Premium (₹5.26M), indicating high claim exposure and risk imbalance
Health insurance contributes the highest premium (~1.36M), showing balanced product distribution

👥 Customer Insights
Gender distribution is nearly equal:
Female: 32.48%
Male: 33.54%
Other: 33.98%
Highest policy concentration in 60+ age group (1888 policies) → key target segment
Lower adoption in younger segments → growth opportunity

📉 Trend Analysis
Premium growth shows high fluctuations over time
Indicates inconsistent revenue performance
Highlights need for better forecasting and planning strategies

🚀 Business Impact
Improved reporting efficiency by ~30% through centralized dashboards
Enabled real-time tracking of KPIs and performance metrics
Identified critical gaps in payment success and claim exposure
Supported data-driven decision-making for business optimization

💡 Recommendations
Implement automated alerts for policy lapses to improve customer retention
Optimize payment collection workflows to reduce failure rates
Use dashboards for weekly performance monitoring
Align individual KPIs with branch-level goals

🔗 Conclusion
This project transforms fragmented insurance data into actionable insights, enabling better visibility, performance tracking, and strategic decision-making.
