# 📊 Insurance Analysis: Branch Performance & Sales Dashboard


> An internship project: a unified analytics solution to monitor branch KPIs, track sales targets, and drive data-informed decisions in the insurance domain.


## 🔹 Overview
This project integrates data from multiple sources into interactive dashboards that give real-time visibility into branch performance across **New**, **Cross-Sell** and **Renewal** business lines, improving reporting efficiency and business insight.

## ⚠️ Problem Statement
The organization lacked a unified view to track and compare branch-level performance. Decision-makers faced:
- No real-time tracking of branch-level KPIs
- Fragmented data across Excel sheets and CRM systems
- No clear alignment between individual targets and actual achievements
- Inability to monitor meetings, opportunities and win ratios
- Limited visibility into invoice count and conversion ratios
- Difficulty identifying top performers vs. lagging branches
- Delayed reporting and misaligned goals

## 🎯 Objective
Build an interactive dashboard using Power BI, Tableau and Excel to centralize data, automate performance tracking and provide real-time decision support.

| # | Focus | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **Branch Monitoring** | Track revenue targets vs. achievement per branch and account executive across business streams |
| 2 | **Sales Funnel Tracking** | Monitor opportunity pipelines, stages and win ratios at individual and branch level |
| 3 | **Strategic Decisions** | Give corporate teams drill-down insights to identify high performers and coaching areas |

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **MySQL** | Database querying and data extraction |
| **Power BI** | Interactive dashboards and KPI tracking |
| **Tableau** | Visual analytics and trend analysis |
| **MS Excel** | Data cleaning, pivot analysis and modeling |

## 📂 Data Sources
1. Target Sheets
2. Brokerage & Fees Records
3. Invoice Reports
4. Meetings Data
5. CRM Opportunities

## 📊 Key Features
- **Branch performance monitoring:** revenue targets vs. actuals at branch and individual level
- **Sales funnel analysis:** pipeline, stages, win ratios and conversion efficiency
- **KPI dashboard:** Policy Count, Customer Count, Claim Amount, Premium Amount, Payment Success %
- **Filters:** year and gender slicers, policy type and status breakdowns
- **SQL layer:** queries such as Total Claim Amount for extraction and validation


## 📈 Key Findings
| KPI | Value |
|-----|-------|
| Total Policy Count | **5,000** |
| Total Customers | **3,148** |
| Total Claim Amount | **₹251M** |
| Total Premium | **₹5.26M** |
| Payment Success Rate | **61.81%** (38.2% failed) |

- **Retention challenge:** Active (33.6%), Lapsed (32.8%) and Terminated (33.56%) policies are almost equally split
- **Product mix:** Health leads policy count (1,316), but all policy types are closely matched
- **Collection gaps:** a 38.2% payment failure rate signals process inefficiencies
- **Risk exposure:** claim amount far exceeds premium, calling for a risk review
- **Premium trend:** premium growth shows high fluctuation over the years

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Implement **automated alerts** for lapsing policies to improve retention
2. Strengthen **payment collection workflows** to reduce the ~38% failure rate
3. Use Power BI **drill-down** to identify underperforming branches weekly
4. Set **individual KPI targets** aligned to branch-level goals via CRM integration



## 🔗 Conclusion
This dashboard bridges the gap between fragmented data and strategic clarity, enabling accountability, transparency and growth across all branches.
